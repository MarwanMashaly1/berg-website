WHAT IS BERG

Everything your group needs
to actually meet up.

🔗

Circles

Join groups built around your interests — hiking, food, music, sport. Meet people worth knowing before you ever meet them in person.

📅

Motives

Create or join real plans with a title, time, and place. No more endless "we should hang" threads that go nowhere. Just show up.

📸

Memories

After every outing, add photos, rate the vibe, and build a shared memory card. The app that lives after the moment, not just before it.

HOW IT WORKS

Three steps to a real night out.

1

Join Circles

Pick circles that match your interests and meet people who are actually into the same things you are.

2

Create Motives

Post a plan — dinner Thursday, hike Saturday, spontaneous thing tonight. Invite the circle, see who's in.

3

Make Memories

Go. Do the thing. Then capture it — photos, ratings, vibe tags. Your social life, documented.
