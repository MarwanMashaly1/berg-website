The core loop

Four steps. Ten seconds of effort.
A real hangout on the other side.

No proposer tax. The match surfaces asymmetrically, so nobody has to be the one who reaches out first.

Everyone gets the same daily prompt. Pick one of 3–4 options in ten seconds. Add a line if you feel like it.

The problem Berg solves
The moment right before you open WhatsApp, stuck on what to say or what to propose, is where friendships quietly drift. Berg fills that gap for you.

Everything, kept small on purpose

Depth over breadth
🔵
Circles

Private friend groups with a 6-character join code, custom emoji, and an auto-created group chat. No public followers, ever.

🗺️
Motives

Nine plan types with real venue suggestions, date voting, RSVPs and a reminder two hours before. Planning without the group-chat chaos.

💬
Follow-up DMs

1:1 messages between confirmed friends: text, image, GIF, tied to the prompt you both answered. Context comes built in.

🧭
Friend-of-friend

Suggestions weighted 60% on mutual friends: only people already inside your circles. Zero-mutual strangers never appear.

🟢
Availability

A quiet status ("down to hang", "busy", "ask me") so friends know when you're actually free without anyone asking.

🎞️
Memory cards

Photos, vibe tags and ratings from every real hang: an artifact you keep that gently resurfaces at T+14 days.
